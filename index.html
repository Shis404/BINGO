🎲 How to Play BINGO 25

1

Arrange Numbers 1 to 25

Shuffle randomly, or clear the grid and tap boxes in order to place 1–25 anywhere you like. Tap any two numbers to swap them!

2

Call Numbers Verbally & Cross

Take turns saying a number out loud with your friend. Both players tap that number on their own screen to cross it off.

3

Complete 5 Lines for B-I-N-G-O!

Every full row, column, or diagonal of 5 crossed numbers unlocks the next letter: B → I → N → G → O. First to complete all 5 lines wins!
